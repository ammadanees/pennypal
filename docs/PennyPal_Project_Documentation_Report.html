1. Executive Summary & Problem Definition

1.1 Background & Necessity (SRS Section 1.1)

Financial literacy is a critical yet routinely neglected life skill among tertiary, undergraduate, and vocational students. Upon entering college or university, students receive disposable funds from allowances, scholarships, student loans, stipends, internships, or part-time employment. However, the vast majority possess little to no formal training in personal budgeting, cash flow tracking, or disciplined savings. Daily micro-transactions—such as cafeteria lunches, transportation, university photocopying, study materials, digital subscriptions, campus events, and social outings—frequently deplete student funds early in the academic month. Traditional tracking methods (such as physical notebooks, paper diaries, or generic desktop spreadsheets) suffer from severe limitations:
  • High Friction: Manual entry in spreadsheets is cumbersome on mobile devices, leading to rapid user abandonment.
  • Lack of Timely Feedback: Notebooks and spreadsheets cannot send real-time threshold warnings when spending nears budget caps.
  • Absence of Pedagogical Guidance: Existing accounting tools present sterile ledgers without explaining financial fundamentals (e.g., the 50/30/20 rule, emergency buffer funds, needs vs. wants).

1.2 Proposed Solution (SRS Section 1.2)

PennyPal is a specialized, responsive, cross-platform personal finance and educational platform engineered specifically for students. It combines:
  1. Frictionless Expense & Income Tracking: Fast entry, multi-mode payment categorization (Cash, Card, Bank, Digital Wallet), and split-bill calculation.
  2. Intelligent Receipt Parsing: Dual-pipeline Gemini 3.8 Flash Vision OCR paired with an instant deterministic regex/keyword fallback.
  3. Proactive Budget Guardrails: Real-time budget tracking with proactive threshold alerts (warning at 80% and over-budget triggers).
  4. Milestone-Driven Savings Goals: Visual goal tracking with automated target timelines and celebration milestones (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%).
  5. Interactive Financial Literacy Academy: An integrated curriculum covering budgeting, savings, emergency funds, debt avoidance, and student life hacks.
  6. Academic Statement Generation: Multi-page PDF statements with executive summaries, spending charts, and itemized transaction ledgers ready for printing, cloud archiving, or CSV export.
  7. Role-Separated Administration: A dedicated administrative portal for managing student accounts, moderating support queries, viewing application analytics, and updating curriculum content.

2.2 System Constraints (SRS Section 1.5)

  • Non-Banking Clause: PennyPal is strictly an educational personal finance management tool. In accordance with SRS Section 1.5, it does not connect to real bank accounts, execute live fund transfers, store real credit card CVV/PIN numbers, or provide regulated investment advice.
  • Real-Time Notification Processing: Spending alerts fire instantly upon transaction entry when category consumption reaches or exceeds the threshold.
  • Cross-Platform Parity: Unified Flutter 3.24+ codebase supporting Android phones, Android tablets, iOS devices, and desktop/web browsers.
  • Offline Resilience: All transactions, budgets, cards, and goals are persisted locally first via Firestore offline caching or MemoryRecordStore with automatic cloud synchronization upon reconnection.
  • AI Latency & Fallback: Gemini AI invocations feature strict timeouts and immediately degrade to deterministic keyword-matching engines if offline or rate-limited.

10. Assumptions, Security & Scope Boundaries

10.1 Key Assumptions

  1. Academic Context: The primary users are students who manage personal pocket money, allowances, and campus stipends.
  2. Device Hardware: Target mobile devices are assumed to possess camera capabilities for receipt scanning, and minimum 2GB RAM.
  3. Internet Connectivity: PennyPal functions both fully offline (caching all records locally) and online (syncing via Firestore when an internet connection is established).

10.2 Security & Data Privacy

  • Row-Level Security: Cloud Firestore security rules (firestore.rules) enforce strict user isolation: students can only read and write their own documents under users/{uid}/*. Administrators are restricted from viewing students' private transaction records.
  • Credential Storage: User authentication is handled via cryptographically signed Firebase JWTs; no plain-text passwords or secret API keys are stored on the client.
  • No Financial Liabilities: Because real financial credentials (e.g. CVV numbers, bank routing credentials) are never stored, user exposure to financial risk is zero.
